« There were nights when the Moon was full
and very, very low, and the tide was so high
that the Moon missed a ducking in the sea

« Climb up on the Moon? Of course we did. »

As documented in The Distance to the Moon, Q was part of a crew that went up to the moon to harvest moon milk. The time was before the Great Separation: the moon and the earth were so close, that the crew would row out to the sea and climb onto the moon with a ladder.

Boat Fragment

The boat was constructed from empty plastic bottles. The crew has tied various inflated objects to the boat to keep it afloat. These buoys and weights were adjusted according to the tides.

« We used to go out with those little rowboats,
long and light, tied together from bottles.
They held quite a few of us: me, Captain Vhdvhd,
 his wife, my deaf cousin, and sometimes little Xlthlx. »

Dress Fragment

Little Xlthlx amused herself by getting caught between the moon and the ocean. Her dress was assembled from plastic sheets that bloomed like a flower when suspended midair. In her flight, schools of faunas have melted into her dress and sealed themselves into little pockets of plastic.

« We rowed hard, to keep up with the child:
the Moon ran off in her ellipse, dragging that
swarm of marine fauna through the sky,
and a train of long, entwined seaweeds,
and Xlthlx hanging there in the midst. »

« Every shiny fish is floating, floating; and every dark fish is at the bottom, at the bottom of the sea ... »

Nettles of feelings grew under the moon. In the long voyages, Q had fallen in love with the captain’s wife - Mrs.Vhdvhd, but Mrs.Vhdvhd was in love with Q’s deaf cousin, who was only intimate with the moon. As Q recounted, she had even seen the Moon come toward her cousin, as he held out his hands.

Ladder Fragment

The ladder was tied together from pvc pipes, aluminum pipes and wood. During the voyage, the crew would keep the ladder floating next to the boat. During the Great Separation, the ladder became just short of reaching the moon, resulting in Q and Mrs.Vhdvhd's estrangement on the moon.

« From the top of the ladder, standing erect
on the last rung, you could just touch the Moon
if you held your arms up. We had taken
the measurements carefully. »

A Bucket

Buckets were constructed from a variety of collapsible materials. At the time, moon-milk was the major source of nutrition. Q and Mrs.Vhdvhd, when stranded on the moon, were sustained by the abundance of moon-milk.

« Now, you will ask me what in the world
we went up on the Moon for. We went to
collect the milk, with a big spoon and a bucket. »

« This had never happened before; the Captain's wife had never gone up on the Moon. »

In one expedition, the crew noticed that the moon was slowly drifting apart from earth. Mrs.Vhdvhd, upon this realization, went up to the moon in anticipation to be stranded with Q’s cousin. But Q's cousin, undeterred by the waning attraction between the earth and moon, returned to the boat. Q herself was stranded with Mrs.Vhdvhd instead.

An Oar

Common materials for oar included electric fan leaves, rackets, plastic pipes, and clothing poles. When embarking on a voyage, the crew always brought extra pieces and ropes, for oars broke easily. During the Great Separation, oars were used to rescue the stranded crew from the Moon.

« This is how we did it: we hurled each spoonful
into the air with both hands, using the spoon
as a catapult. The cheese flew, and if we had
thrown it hard enough, it stuck to the ceiling,
I mean the surface of the sea. Once there,
it floated, and the crew would
use their oars to pull it into the boat. »

A Headdress

Exquisitely built from plastic bottles and cups, the headdress was likely a resonance chamber for Mrs.Vhdvhd, who was a sensational harp player. Mrs.Vhdvhd had likely dropped her headdress when she left for the moon.

« It didn't take me long to realize whom the lady
kept looking at insistently: when my cousin's hands
clasped the satellite, I watched Mrs. Vhdvhd,
and in her eyes I could read the thoughts that
my cousin's familiarity with the Moon was arousing in her. »

« Torn from its earthly soil, my love now knew only the heart-rending nostalgia for what it lacked... »

When the crew finally came to their rescue, Q seized the first chance to return to earth. But none could persuade Mrs.Vhdvhd to leave the moon. She knew that if Q’s cousin loved the moon so much, this was the only way his gaze would follow her. On the moon she remained, as the crew watched the moon drift away.
